**Skill ID:** `call-followup-email`
**Server:** [RingEX Phone](../servers/ringex-phone.md)

Pulls a call from the user's RingEX call history, resolves who was on the other end and their email address, drafts a follow-up that references the call (using the AI call summary/transcript when one exists), and sends it through whatever email tool is actually available — or hands back a ready-to-send draft if none is connected.
